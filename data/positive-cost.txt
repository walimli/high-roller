# A virtue with a cost. One entry per line.
generous, and then broke
loyal past the point it is good for them
honest in rooms where honesty starts fights
patient until people mistake it for agreement
brave in a way that skips the exit
kind to strangers, short with their own
meticulous, and late because of it
protective, and hard to leave alone
hopeful enough to ignore a warning
fair, even when mercy would have been kinder
curious about things that are none of their business
humble in a way that hides real skill
forgiving, including people who will do it again
dutiful, including to a bad order
cheerful at the wrong funerals
independent to the point of not asking for help
polite even to people who do not deserve it
steady, and therefore slow to change their mind
devoted to a craft and neglectful of meals and sleep
merciful, and then surprised when it is used
open-handed with advice nobody requested
principled about small rules
warm, and bad at endings
self-reliant, and secretive about being hurt
idealistic about offices that have already failed them
generous with time, and resentful afterward
brave for other people, careless with themselves
truthful about facts, silent about feelings
hospitable past the point the larder can bear
faithful to a teacher who was wrong
